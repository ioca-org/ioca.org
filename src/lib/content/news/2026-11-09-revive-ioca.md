Founded in 1932 by a group of 14 collegiate outing clubs across the Northeast, IOCA was formed with a simple goal: to promote intercollegiate outdoor activities, organize group trips, be a resource to clubs, and create long-lasting friendships among students and alumni. For many, IOCA remains a vivid memory thanks to trips like the Fall Lake George trip and the Spring Conference. 

Unfortunately, COVID-19 became a barrier for many clubs. Trips became impossible to run, leading to low membership. As a result, many clubs became disconnected from IOCA altogether. With both IOCA’s Spring Conference and Fall Lake George trips canceled, IOCA struggled to stay afloat.

In early 2026, the Outing Club at Cornell University had a vision: to restart IOCA. The vision was composed of two main ideas. First, to create a community aiding clubs in joint trips with nearby clubs. These trips could include day hikes, backpacking trips, and others. Second, to bring together a forum of members, allowing clubs all over the US and Canada to share expertise with each other.

If you are interested in this mission, we encourage you to join us! Here’s where you can help:

- **Collegiate outing clubs**: If you are interested in joining IOCA and working with other clubs in your area, whether that’s just a day trip or something larger, fill out the form on our website at [ioca.org/join](/join).

- **Alumni**: If you have history to share or are interested in helping reach out to other clubs, please fill out the form on our website at [ioca.org/join](/join).

If you have any questions, please reach out to us at [info@ioca.org](mailto:info@ioca.org)
